Holdpace privacy policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Holdpace is made by Naturamine Studios ("we"). This policy explains what information Holdpace uses, where it is kept and the choices you have.

In short: your runs stay on your phone. Holdpace has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we never sell your data. We run no server that receives your runs, so we cannot see them.

What Holdpace uses and why

  • Location during a run. Holdpace uses your phone's precise location (GPS) only while a run is in progress, to measure your distance and pace and to draw your route. It does not track you at any other time.
  • Motion and steps. If you allow physical activity access, Holdpace counts steps during a run for cadence and treadmill distance.
  • Air pressure. On phones with a barometer, Holdpace reads it during a run to work out hills.
  • What you enter. Your workouts, plans, goals, comfortable pace and settings.
  • Run history. Each run's route, times, pace, splits, cadence and the alerts that played.
  • Pace Coach. If you use it, Holdpace keeps your fitness level and how it changed, your training days and weekly distance, your goal race, and how each run felt (an optional rating from Very easy to Very hard). If you skip a session or mark a run as cut short, the reason you pick, for example "Sick or injured", is kept too, so the coach doesn't count it against you. All of this is worked out on your phone.

Heart rate and runs from your watch

Both are off until you turn them on. Holdpace only ever reads from Health Connect. It never writes to it.

  • Heart rate. Holdpace can read heart rate from Health Connect (where apps such as Samsung Health, Fitbit and Garmin Connect store what your watch or strap records), for the time of each of your runs only. It looks when you open the app after a run, for up to a week afterwards. If more than one app saved heart rate for the same run, Holdpace uses the one that saved the run, or the one with the most readings.
  • What it keeps. For each run: the average, the highest reading, drift, your heart rate along the route (about once a second when your watch recorded it), the average for each section and your recovery between hard efforts. This is stored with that run on your phone, and in your own Google backup if that is on (see below).
  • How far back it can look. Health Connect only lets Holdpace see readings from the 30 days before you first allowed access, and anything after that. Older runs may have no heart rate.
  • What it is used for. Showing it to you, and letting Pace Coach adjust your training. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not shared, and no human reads it.
  • Runs from your watch. If you turn on "Count runs from your watch", Holdpace reads your running sessions from Health Connect (only runs, from the last 30 days) so they show in History and count for your plan and Pace Coach. For each session it keeps the start time, length, distance, your speed along the run, your heart rate if heart rate is also on, the name of the app that saved it and whether it was a treadmill run. It does not keep the session's title or notes, and it reads nothing else.
  • Turning off. Turning either setting off in Holdpace stops it reading, and offers to remove what it has already stored. You can also turn them off in Health Connect.
  • Live heart rate from a sensor. If you pair a Bluetooth heart rate sensor in Settings (a chest strap, or a watch in heart rate broadcast mode), Holdpace reads it during your runs only. It shows on the run screen, your coach can use it, and it is kept with the run like heart rate from Health Connect. It stays on your phone like the rest, and in your own Google backup if that is on. Bluetooth is used only to find and connect to the sensor, never to work out where you are. Tap Forget in Settings to stop.

Holdpace's use of Health Connect data follows the Health Connect permissions policy, including its limited use requirements.

Health-related notes (how runs felt, skip reasons, "unwell" marks and heart rate) are treated like the rest of your runs. They stay on your phone, are included in your export unless you turn on "Keep health notes private" in Settings, and are removed by "Delete all my data". They are never used for advertising and never shared.

Where your data is kept

On your phone, in Holdpace's private storage. Heart rate and everything else Holdpace stores stays on your phone, and in your own Google backup if that is on.

If Android backup is on for your phone, Google backs up Holdpace's data to your Google account, as it does for other apps. This includes your Pace Coach data, how runs felt, skip reasons, heart rate stored with each run and runs imported from your watch. Runs in progress and your Pro subscription status are not backed up. You control backup in your phone's settings.

Services that receive some information

  • Google Maps. Run maps use Google Maps. To show a map, your phone downloads map images for the area being viewed, so Google receives that area and your device's internet address, under the Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy). You can turn map backgrounds off in Settings, and routes are then drawn without any download.
  • Open-Meteo (weather). With Holdpace Pro, when an outdoor run starts the app asks Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) for the current temperature and dew point, so it can offer an easier pace on hot, humid days. It sends a rough location, rounded to about 10 km, once per run. Open-Meteo receives that rough location and your device's internet address. No account or other details are sent. Nothing is sent without Pro or on indoor runs.
  • Google Play. If you subscribe to Holdpace Pro, Google Play handles the payment and we receive confirmation that your subscription is active, never your card or payment details. Asking for a rating uses Google Play's own rating window. "Give a friend a free month" opens your phone's share menu with a Google Play promo link, and Holdpace doesn't see who you share it with.
  • Sharing you choose. If you share a run card or export a file, it goes wherever you send it. The same goes for workout files you send to your watch. Shared routes leave out the start and end (200 m by default, set in Settings) so they don't show where you live.

We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics services.

Data that goes overseas

Holdpace keeps your data on your phone, but two services above are run from other countries. Google (Maps and Play) handles data in the United States and other countries where Google operates. Open-Meteo handles requests in Europe. Each receives only what is described above, and handles it under its own privacy policy.

Your choices and rights

  • See and export your data. Settings, then Export all my data, gives you every run, route and setting as GPX, CSV and JSON files, with a guide to every column. Runs imported from your watch are included. The export includes heart rate unless "Keep health notes private" is on.
  • Delete your data. Settings, then Delete all my data, removes every run, route, plan, goal, Pace Coach history, how-it-felt rating, skip reason, run imported from your watch, copied heart rate, saved map image, exported file and learnt setting from your phone. Heart rate stored in Health Connect itself stays there, under your control in Health Connect. Whether you have a Holdpace Pro subscription is kept, as Google Play confirms it anyway. Uninstalling Holdpace also removes its data.
  • Permissions. You can turn off location, activity or notification access in Android settings at any time. Some features stop working without them.

Under Australian privacy law, and laws such as the GDPR where they apply, you can ask what information we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it. Because Holdpace keeps your data on your phone, we usually hold none.

Children

Holdpace is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

Contact and complaints

Questions, or a request about your information: support@naturaminestudios.com. We will reply within 30 days.

If you are in Australia and are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992. If you are elsewhere, you can contact your local privacy authority.
